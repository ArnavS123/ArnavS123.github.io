Why x7 instead of x0?

In an early version of my Digital Logic Sim CPU datapath, I had no NOP detector, so a NOP (all-zero instruction) could write to x0, the traditional zero register. I moved the zero register to x7 in order to keep NOPs encoded as all zeros. My finalized CPU datapath can now detect NOPs, so this workaround is no longer necessary. However, I kept x7 as my zero register because it makes the design feel more unique and personal.
